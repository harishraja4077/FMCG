FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about Stackly.

How do I become a Stackly retail partner?
Simply fill out the contact form above or call our sales team at +91 70107 92745. Our partner acquisition team will get in touch within 24 hours to discuss the onboarding process, pricing, and logistics.
What is the minimum order quantity?
Our minimum order quantity varies by category. For general FMCG products, the MOQ starts at ₹10,000. Contact our sales team for specific category-wise MOQ details and bulk discount information.
Do you deliver across India?
Yes! We currently serve 28 states across India through our 15+ regional distribution hubs. We also offer cold chain logistics for perishable products to ensure freshness throughout the supply chain.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), UPI payments, credit/debit cards, and cheque payments. For regular partners, we also offer convenient credit terms of 30-60 days based on the partnership tier.
Can I return or exchange products?
Yes, we have a hassle-free return policy. Damaged or defective products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Our customer support team will arrange a pickup and either a replacement or full refund.
How do I track my order?
Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can also log into our digital platform to view real-time order status, estimated delivery time, and delivery confirmation.
