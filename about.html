Who We Are

Building India's Strongest FMCG Network

Founded in 2000, Stackly began as a small distribution firm in Mumbai. Today, we are a pan-India FMCG powerhouse serving over 15,000 retail outlets with 2,500+ products from 500+ brand partners. Our commitment to quality, reliability, and innovation has made us the preferred distribution partner for leading brands.

We leverage cutting-edge technology and a vast logistics network to ensure timely delivery and complete satisfaction for our partners and end consumers.

Journey

Our Timeline

Key milestones that shaped who we are today.

2000

Founded in Mumbai

Stackly started as a small FMCG distribution firm with a team of 5 people and a vision to transform Indian retail.

2005

First 1000 Retail Outlets

Expanded our distribution network to cover over 1,000 retail outlets across Maharashtra and Gujarat.

2010

Pan-India Expansion

Opened regional hubs in Delhi, Chennai, and Kolkata, establishing a truly national distribution presence.

2015

Digital Transformation

Launched our proprietary digital ordering platform, making it easier for retailers to manage inventory.

2020

15,000+ Outlets Served

Reached the milestone of serving 15,000+ retail outlets across 28 states with 2,500+ products.

2025

Asia's FMCG Leader

Recognized as one of Asia's top FMCG distribution companies with plans for global expansion.
